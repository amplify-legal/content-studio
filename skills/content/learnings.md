## Starter rules (generic, learned on a working system, keep or replace with your own)

These came from real runs on the system this pack was built from. They are written without names or dates. Each one generalised beyond its first video. Delete any that turn out wrong for you; a rule you never tested is a guess.

- **Ask questions that tease the creator's brain, not questions that collect answers.** The door is in the business of boosting creativity. The machine's offers are sparks, never endpoints, and the deliverable of a review pass is questions that pull the creator's version out, then lines written together. (Home: `SKILL.md` section 3.)

- **On-screen engagement is co-created.** When the video needs something to keep viewers watching (a persistent on-screen tracker, a highlighted criterion, a running total), do not propose it. Ask "what should the viewer see on screen that keeps them engaged?" and build it together. (Home: `SKILL.md` section 3.)

- **A story is picked for the viewer's situation, never for its drama.** Two rounds of story options built on the most dramatic moments of a creator's life were rejected, because the viewer would never be in that situation, so the story could not help them avoid the mistake. Before any brief, caption or script reaches for a story, ask: would someone in this audience see themselves in this? (Home: `references/interview-stages.md`, the brief stage.)

- **The CTA is asked per video, never assumed from a framework.** A framework's default slot (a mid-video ask at about a third of the way in, a free resource) is only the default the question offers. One creator settled on a standing early ask plus an end CTA chosen per video by its purpose; another wanted the only ask at the end. Ask what the end CTA is for THIS video. (Home: `references/interview-stages.md`, step 7.)

- **Recording planning is a structured brainstorm on the script map.** Fill the map one box at a time: free talk about the box, the machine sorts the lines in, then asks only what the box lacks. Show the map on a page during the session. Mark this untested until your own first run reviews it. (Home: `SKILL.md` section 3.)

- **Watch the tape before the first question on anything derived from a recording.** Reading a transcript plus a handful of frames is reading, not watching. The watch pass routinely changes the question you were about to ask, and twice found privacy problems (an attendee grid, a private sidebar) that would have shipped. (Home: `SKILL.md` section 2.)

- **When every option in a batch is a way to lose, the option set is the failure, not their indecision.** Build the way out before asking anyone to choose among compromises. The retrieval half of the work is where the creative work hides. (Home: `SKILL.md` section 3.)

- **"B-roll" is the wrong word for generated video, and metaphor is the trap.** Generated video is a story animation: a visual aid for the exact line being said. Mood footage failed, then elegant metaphors failed (glass rods for hours, a paper stack for invoices), because they looked expensive and explained nothing. The test: quote the script line the animation illustrates. If you cannot point at one, it is b-roll. Test it with the sound off. (Home: `content/taste/story-animations.md`.)

- **A recording block with nothing shaped behind it leads every plan.** It is a content decision, not a calendar row. (Home: `SKILL.md` section 2.)

- **Packaging before script.** The title and the thumbnail promise are shaped before the script is written. A guideline, not a gate on drafting, and the constitution's 4 packaging questions still decide when a video is ready to record. (Home: `content/PRINCIPLES.md` section 4.)

- **A deletion is a decision.** Never rebuild killed work from version control or from a live document that still points at it; fix the pointer and add a row to `content/graveyard.md`. (Home: `references/intents.md`.)

- **The retro is never self-review.** A separate reviewer sub-agent reads the whole conversation and the session hands its list over unedited. (Home: `SKILL.md` section 8.)
