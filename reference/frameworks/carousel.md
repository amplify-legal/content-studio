# Framework, the carousel

> One carousel per pillar video. An important piece, not necessarily a daily
> one. The copy comes out of the pillar's own transcript, never out of thin
> air.

---

## Shape

6 to 8 slides, one idea per slide, all of it mined from what was actually said
on the recording.

1. **Cover.** The promise as a designed card. Big claim, your display type, no
   clutter.

   The cover doubles as the **style reference for every other slide**, so slide
   2 onward inherits the look automatically. That one trick is most of what
   makes a set look like a set, whether you are generating the slides or
   building them by hand.

2. **Slides 2 to 6 or 7.** One point each, minimal words. The on-screen-words
   rule from `content/taste/editing-checklist.md` applies here too: keywords and structure,
   never paragraphs. Somebody is reading this with a thumb over half the
   screen.

3. **Last slide.** One call to action, per the week's mix, plus where to find
   the full video.

---

## Style

Your brand kit throughout, and the same file works on every surface that takes
a carousel. What changes per platform is how it gets posted, not how it looks.

Pin these down once in `content/brand.md`, and never decide them per
carousel:

- the ground and the ink color
- one accent, used for the claim and the call to action
- one secondary
- a display face, a label face, a body face
- the slide size, and whether the last slide is a different shape

Numbers formatted the way your audience reads them.

---

## Caption

The post caption follows the rules of the account it goes out from, not the
rules of whoever assembled the slides. If 2 people post from the business, the
caption belongs to the account owner's voice file. Emoji policy is per surface,
because the same caption reads differently on a professional feed and a
lifestyle one.

---

## Retro

2 things are worth reading at the weekly review: which slide people stopped on,
which most platforms will tell you, and whether the carousel drove profile
visits.

The build path (generated, rendered from HTML, or assembled by hand) gets
settled by results, never by preference. Try all 3 on real posts before
declaring one of them the way you do it.
