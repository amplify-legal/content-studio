# Framework, the send artifact

> The one-pager built from each week's pillar and sent by name to about a dozen
> specific people. This is the piece that does not wait for an algorithm to
> decide whether anybody sees it.
>
> It is also newsletter-shaped from day 1 on purpose, so that launching a
> newsletter later is a switch rather than a project. Write it as if it already
> is one.

---

## The bar

**"Thought of you" or it does not ship.**

Every artifact has to be sendable to a named person with those 3 words in front
of it, because of who they are and what they are dealing with this month. Not
"this might interest you". Thought of you.

If the week's pillar cannot produce that, the artifact does not ship that week,
and the honest read is that the gate failed further upstream when the topic got
picked.

---

## Structure, one page

1. **The claim.** The pillar's promise in one line, anchored to a number where
   you can do that honestly.
2. **The meat.** The 3 to 5 load-bearing points from the video, written to
   stand on their own. Somebody who never clicks through still gets the value.
   This is the part that makes it forwardable.
3. **The proof.** One real result or one real moment. Only numbers from your
   `content/taste/stories.md`, and your anonymity rules apply the same way they do on camera.
4. **The video.** A tracked link, framed as the full walkthrough.
5. **One call to action**, per the week's mix. Usually the free thing. The
   booking link only when the week's mix calls for it, or when you already
   offered it to that person out loud.

Email capture lives on it from the first one, because retrofitting that later
means the first 40 of these produced no list.

---

## Tracked links

Every link carries parameters naming the artifact and the group it went to, so
that when somebody books you can trace it back to the piece that did it.
Booking links get the same treatment.

Without this, the weekly numbers are a vibe. With it, you can answer "which
piece produced a conversation" with a fact.

---

## The send

It goes out by name, with a personal opening line per person. One person builds
it, one person sends it, and nothing sends automatically.

A list of 12 names with 12 openers is a reasonable weekly ask. A list of 200
with one opener is a newsletter pretending to be a personal note, and everybody
can tell.

A follow-up after a call can carry the same link with a 3 line summary. That is
a sales message, not a content placement, and it is written as one.

---

## The return

**This loop is the point of the whole artifact.** Everything above is just
making something good enough to justify it.

Whoever engages back, a reply, a meeting, a mention, gets logged wherever you
keep a record of that relationship, the same day. Their questions feed next week's ideas. That is what
turns the artifact into a conversation that repeats every week with the same 12
people.

---

## Retro

Reply rate and conversations traced back land in your `content/scoreboard.md`. What
made a specific named person actually answer gets written down here, dated.
That is the only part of this that compounds.
