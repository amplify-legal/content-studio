# Comments and the pinned comment: every piece asks

> A video with thousands of views and zero comments usually never asked for one. This file holds the rule and the lines. Every option is proposed until you pick it, and nothing is posted or pinned without your go.

## The rule

<Your words. A starting default: every piece of content asks the viewer, in the comments, what they want next and what they did not understand.>

## What the pinned comment carries

A starting default, in this order:

1. **Value first.** A free thing, or your answer to a question.
2. **The comment ask, as a real question.** What they want next, or what they heard and did not understand. Never a keyword to type back: keyword bait reads as a funnel.
3. **The next step, 1 link.** <your booking or resource link>

Before it is pinned, every link is fetched in that session, and a booking link is checked for real open slots. A page that loads proves nothing about availability. A pinned comment stays up for months, so its links are re-checked at each weekly review.

## How it gets used

- Every script closes on one of the lines above, in your own words on the day.
- Every video carries the pinned comment. Pinning is usually by hand in the platform.
- Comments that arrive are read at the weekly review as the list of what to teach next. The few who comment are often high intent.
