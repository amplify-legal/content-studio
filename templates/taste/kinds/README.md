# The kinds: one sheet per kind of video

> Taste is data. A tutorial, a masterclass, a short, a podcast and a live are judged by different bars, and an editor who treats them as one kind of video will cut a tutorial like a podcast. This folder keeps one sheet per kind: what that kind is for in the viewer's mind, the frame the creator sets, the opening, the pacing, the packaging, the CTA and where its edit rules live.
>
> The sheets here are **starting defaults** from a working channel, each line written as a default with its reason. Keep, change or strike any line, and replace the reasons with your own receipts as they arrive. The `director` skill reads the kind's sheet before it asks its questions; `cut-review`, the content door, `shorts-editor` and `long-form-editor` read it first for any cut or recording of that kind.

## Which kind is this

| Kind | What the viewer came for | Default length | Where it usually posts | Sheet | The edit file |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Tutorial | To do one thing on their own data by the end, following you | 8 to 14 minutes (`content/frameworks/youtube-pillar.md`, Shape) | YouTube long form | `tutorial.md` | `content/taste/editing-rules.md`, `content/taste/editing-checklist.md` |
| Masterclass | The whole job, end to end, one definitive resource | 45 to 90 minutes | YouTube long form | `masterclass.md` | same as tutorial, plus the assembly rule |
| Short | One lesson, a lot of value, under 60 seconds | under 60 s (`content/taste/shorts-formats.md`) | YouTube Shorts, Instagram, TikTok, LinkedIn | `short.md` | `content/taste/shorts-formats.md`, `content/frameworks/short.md`, `content/taste/clip-bar.md` |
| Podcast | A conversation with a guest, the guest's world through your lens | about 25 to 45 minutes after the cut | YouTube, the podcast feed | `podcast.md` | `content/reference/podcast-edit.md`, `content/taste/podcast-hosting.md` |
| Live | A conversation in front of a room, topics made tangible | 45 to 60 minutes live, cut to under 15 | a live platform, then YouTube | `live.md` | `content/reference/workshop-edit.md` |

When a recording is none of these (a case study, a carousel, a written post), say so and read `content/frameworks/` instead. A sixth sheet is a question for your next kinds review, not a sheet yet.

## The sections every sheet has, in this order

1. **What it is for** (in the viewer's mind)
2. **The frame you set** (how you come across)
3. **The opening**
4. **Pacing and length**
5. **Packaging** (title shape, thumbnail direction)
6. **The CTA**
7. **The edit** (a pointer to the kind's edit file and the rules there that define the kind, never a copy)
8. **Rules** (your rulings, dated, with receipts; only a person moves a line here)
9. **Lines from the calls** (newest first, verbatim, a receipt on every line)

## How a sheet changes

- Sections 1 to 8 change by hand, on your word or your editor's, dated, with the recording or call it came from.
- Section 9 is append-only, newest first, one bullet per line, in this shape:
  `- YYYY-MM-DD · <tag: frame, cut, pacing, title, thumbnail, voice or kind> · "<the line, verbatim>" · receipt: <the call or recording, and where in it> · <who said it>`
- After a review call or an edit session, append the creator's lines about that kind to its sheet with a receipt. Refuse a line with no receipt, and refuse a duplicate.
- **Nothing moves from section 9 to section 8 by itself.** A line that has appeared 3 times is put to the creator to promote: "this has come up 3 times, is it a rule now?" The creator rules; the machine never promotes.
- A simple check worth running before a review session: every bullet in section 9 has a receipt, and the newest lines since the last session are read first.
