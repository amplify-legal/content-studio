## Starting defaults, change them

### Rule 1: captions are phrase-level and build in sync

Burned-in captions of about 5 to 10 words, sentence case, built word by word in sync with speech, the current word highlighted. The 1 to 2 word ALL CAPS preset is out: it forces a cut every word, flattens emphasis, and cannot hold a phrase, which is how ideas land. Big hard-cut title cards are allowed for a cold-open flourish only, never for talking lines. Position and size floors for vertical are in `shorts-formats.md`.

- Grounded in: <your clip id, timestamp, what the caption did on that beat>

### Rule 2: the hook stands alone

The first 30 to 40 seconds work as a self-contained unit: problem or wrong framing, the turn, the promise, and only then who you are. The idea is the hook, not the resume. Cut at the spoken beat that completes the arc, not at a stopwatch.

### Rule 3: the first frame is the packaged promise

Frame 0 is a designed card or a real frame that states the promise, not a cold talking head mid-sentence. It is the only thing a feed scroller is guaranteed to see.

- Grounded in: <...>

### Rule 4: no face in a box on vertical

A presenter inset in a corner works on landscape and wastes a vertical frame. Vertical uses a full-frame speaker or clean full-bleed graphics, with text and visuals where a phone viewer looks.

### Rule 5: music ducks, sound effects punctuate

An instrumental bed well under the voice, ducking when you talk. Sparse sound effects mark real transitions and element entrances. They earn their place, they never sprinkle. <Your level, for example a bed at 2% for shorts.>

### Rule 6: a purposeful visual change every few seconds

Roughly every 1 to 5 seconds, and every change delivers information: a highlight, a proof, a screen. The test for every cut: did this advance the point? If not, hold.

### Technical defaults

- Overlays with transparency: ProRes 4444 `.mov`, matching the timeline frame rate.
- Loudness target around -14 LUFS for social platforms. Subtitles burn in last.
- Confirm the cut plan in plain English with the person before rendering. No auto-upload.
