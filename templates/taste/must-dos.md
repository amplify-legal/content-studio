# The must-dos: what every recording carries

> One standing list, pulled up automatically on every recording plan, every recording session and every launch. The point is one artifact instead of rules scattered across files. Every line names where it came from (a recording, a review, a ruling) so a reader can check it instead of taking it on trust. Rules and guidelines are marked apart: a guideline off 1 example stays a guideline until it repeats.
>
> The lines below are **starting defaults** from a working channel, each one learned the hard way. Keep, change or strike each one, and replace the "Where it came from" column with your own receipts as they arrive. Keep each line's number once it is cited, so references stay stable.

## D. The moment the take ends, while the set is still up

| # | The must-do | Where it came from | Rule or guideline |
|---|---|---|---|
| 18 | **Ask "what did we miss" before anything comes down.** A pickup caught here costs 15 seconds; caught a day later it costs the set. | <...> | Rule |

The questions, as a starting default:

- What did we miss, in substance, beyond the script?
- Is there something we had to add and did not?
- Is there a resource we named and did not show?
- Is there something we absolutely should have said?
- Did we skip an intro beat from section B?

The answer is a page, not a chat reply: pickups to shoot now, things that can be recorded on screen later, every promise made on camera, anything that must not ship, and the smaller notes.
