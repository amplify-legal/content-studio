# On-screen graphics: a board shown beside a live demo on camera

> A graphic you show on camera next to a live tool (a "dashboard", a flow of what the machine built). Read before building any such screen. Taste is data; the builders read this and never restate it.

## Starting defaults, change them

They came from a real review of 3 versions of one screen, where every version carried the whole anatomy (7 sections, 11 exhibits, 5 checks, file names, page references) in small type, and the creator's verdict was that it is not about mashing information into one thing, it is about making it easy to see what is happening and where the human step comes in. Dense is not clear.

- **Clear steps, left to right.** The viewer reads the flow in 2 seconds on a phone-sized frame.
- **Headlines only, much less text.** 2 to 5 words a box. None of the small letters.
- **One big box for where the human comes in**, visibly different, sitting before the final product. The screen exists to carry that one idea.
- **Thick arrows, one direction, high contrast.**
- **Friendly, not childish.**
- **Pass each coded version through an image model with the same instruction** and show the pair, so the simplification the model finds is seen beside the one you drew.
